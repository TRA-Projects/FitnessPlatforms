# 🏋️ FitnessPlatforms

> **FitnessPlatforms** is a comprehensive web application designed to help users manage their overall fitness journey, personalize workout routines, track progress, and support healthy lifestyle habits through an intuitive experience.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **👤 Multi-Role Management:** Dedicated workflows for Administrators, Trainers/Instructors, and Trainees.
* **📋 Personalized Workouts & Programs:** Custom workout plans tailored to individual fitness goals.
* **📈 Progress & Attendance Tracking:** Real-time tracking of attendance, daily tasks, and workout history.
* **🏢 Corporate Integration:** Support for corporate-sponsored training programs and organizations.
* **🎫 Support & Assistance:** Integrated ticketing system for seamless user help and queries.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience
* **Trainees & Fitness Enthusiasts** looking to reach health milestones.
* **Instructors & Personal Trainers** designing structured fitness programs.
* **Corporate Clients** sponsoring wellness initiatives for employees.
